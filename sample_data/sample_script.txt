INT. DINER - NIGHT

MAYA (28) sits alone in a booth, phone glowing. A jukebox in the corner
plays a faint, recognizable riff -- "Sweet Child O' Mine" hums under the
scene.

On the table: a red Solo cup and an unmistakable can of Coca-Cola,
label facing camera.

Across the diner, a TV mounted on the wall plays a muted late-night talk
show. We recognize the host -- a dead ringer for JIMMY FALLON -- mid-laugh.

MAYA
(to herself)
This is the last time I eat at Denny's.

She glances out the window. Across the street, the neon sign of THE
CHATEAU MARMONT flickers against the LA skyline.

CUT TO:

EXT. CHATEAU MARMONT - CONTINUOUS

A black town car pulls up. A figure who looks exactly like ELON MUSK
steps out, trailed by security.
